Casa Terrazza

Concept Development · Visual Direction · Prompt Design · AI Visualization · Editing

This project explores the visualization of a contemporary three-story villa through a series of cinematic architectural scenes. The focus was placed on highlighting the relationship between form, materials, light, and spatial composition while preserving the character of the original design.

Special attention was given to the rooftop terrace, structural massing, façade materials, landscape integration, and the overall atmosphere of the space. Through carefully designed camera movements and lighting studies, the project aims to present the architecture from multiple perspectives and emphasize its visual identity.

The workflow combined AI-assisted image generation, scene development, animation, and post-production techniques to create a cohesive architectural presentation. The result is a visual study that communicates the design language, scale, and spatial experience of the villa in a cinematic format.
